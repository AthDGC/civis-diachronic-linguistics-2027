Frequently asked questions

Is the application still open?

The 2027 student call on CIVIS Agora ran from 1 to 31 October 2026 and has closed. The physical week can still be joined through the Naxos Summer School registration.

Can I take part with no experience of computational linguistics?

Yes. The programme welcomes students from diverse backgrounds, and no prior computational experience is required.

Is there a grant for the week on Naxos?

Students admitted to the programme receive a mobility grant for the Naxos week. Physical mobility in a CIVIS programme is limited to five days.

Do I receive ECTS credits?

Yes, the programme awards 6 ECTS credits. Recognition depends on your home university’s policies; please check with your university’s international office.

Can I take part if my university is not a CIVIS member?

The CIVIS programme is open to students of CIVIS member universities. Students of other universities are welcome at the Naxos Summer School.

Organizing institution

National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, Faculty of Philosophy, Department of English Language and Literature, Division of Language-Linguistics. University Campus, Zografou, 157 84 Athens, Greece. This programme is organized in collaboration with CIVIS partner universities.

About CIVIS

CIVIS is a European Civic University Alliance bringing together universities from across Europe to create an innovative and interdisciplinary space for students, academics and citizens.
