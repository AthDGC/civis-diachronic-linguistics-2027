2026–2027

Program

Phase 1: Online Spring School

Monday 5 – Friday 9 April 2027. This year the online week has fewer lectures and more practice. Short lectures by the course coordinators and invited lecturers from several CIVIS universities open each day, and most of the time goes to guided practical sessions in which students work with historical texts, corpora and digital tools. The daily timetable and the lecturers will be published on this page as they are confirmed.

Phase 2: Naxos Summer School

Monday 5 – Friday 9 July 2027, with arrival on Sunday 4 July. The physical week takes place within the 12th Naxos Summer School on Diachronic Linguistics, at the former Ursuline School in the Venetian Castle (Kastro) of Chora, on the island of Naxos, Greece: lectures, workshops, master classes and a poster session, with up to 6 ECTS credits.

Programme and practical information: Naxos Summer School 2027 website.

Credits

The programme awards 6 ECTS credits. Recognition of these credits depends on your home university’s policies; please check with your university’s international office.
