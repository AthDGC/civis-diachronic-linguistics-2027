Admissions

Apply

The 2027 student call ran on the CIVIS Agora platform from 1 to 31 October 2026 and has closed.

Who could apply

The programme is open to Bachelor’s, Master’s and PhD students enrolled at CIVIS member universities at the time of the programme.

  • Background: linguistics, philology, literature, culture, history, archaeology, digital humanities, computer science, anthropology, or related fields. Basic knowledge of historical linguistics is preferred but not required.
  • A good level of written and spoken English (B2).
  • No prior computational experience is required.

How the application worked

Applicants prepared an academic CV and a motivation letter (500–750 words) and applied through the CIVIS Agora platform. Students admitted to the programme receive a mobility grant for the week on Naxos.

Other ways to join in 2027

The physical week is part of the Naxos Summer School on Diachronic Linguistics, which is open to BA, MA and PhD students and early-career researchers. Registration is open on the summer school website.
